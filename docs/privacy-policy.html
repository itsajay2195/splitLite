Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Baagam ("the app") is a free, offline bill-splitting app. This policy explains our approach to your data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely on your device with no internet connection required.

Data Storage

All data you enter (expenses, names, amounts) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. It never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
